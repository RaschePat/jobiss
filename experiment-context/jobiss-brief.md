# Jobiss Brief — Common Input

## Product
Jobiss(잡이쓰) is an AI career exploration and job-preparation service. Users can register objective career information such as education, training, certifications, projects, portfolio, GitHub, activities, and interests. AI analyzes those records to help users explore suitable roles and job postings.

## Problem
Job seekers often struggle to judge which roles fit them, connect their past experiences to a job, identify gaps, and decide what to prepare next.

## Core Flow
1. Register experiences and career information.
2. AI analyzes the user's signals and possible role fit.
3. Suitable job postings arrive in a mailbox-like experience.
4. The user selects an interesting posting.
5. AI compares the posting with the user's profile.
6. The service explains matching evidence, gaps, and next actions.

## Core Features
- Profile
- AI Analysis
- Job Mailbox
- Job Fit Report
- Next Action

## Target User
Job seekers who are still exploring their direction or who struggle to connect their existing experiences to a role or posting.

## Brand Facts
- Korean name: 잡이쓰
- English name: Jobiss
- Main color: #ADD6FF
- Korean-first service
- The syllable `잡` may be visually emphasized in the wordmark.
- A letter / mailbox metaphor may be used for newly arrived job postings.
