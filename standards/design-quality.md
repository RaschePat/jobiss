# Design Quality Rubric v0.1

Use these criteria as the common definition of quality for Experiment 04.

## Product Communication
- Is the core Jobiss value understandable?
- Is the primary action clear?
- Does the page communicate possibility → evidence → gaps → next action?

## Art Direction
- Is one Visual Thesis perceptible without explanation?
- Are composition, scale, density, whitespace, and eye flow deliberate?
- Does typography contribute to identity and rhythm rather than hierarchy alone?
- Do UI, imagery, color, type, motion, and background belong to one visual world?
- Would the page remain recognizable if generic branding were removed?

## Asset Quality
If bespoke assets are used, assess whether they are necessary, product-specific, compositionally integrated, visually coherent, and free of generic AI/stock character.

## Functional Quality
- Desktop and mobile remain usable.
- No obvious clipping, overflow, broken interactions, or unreadable contrast.
- Core navigation and CTA behavior remain viable.

## Storytelling
- Does the page begin from a recognizable user tension?
- Can the user follow how Jobiss changes uncertainty into evidence and action?
- Does product UI act as evidence rather than automatically dominate the art direction?

This rubric defines what good means. It does not itself require a post-render self-evaluation or revision cycle.
