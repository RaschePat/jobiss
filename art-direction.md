# Art Direction Protocol v0.2

## Purpose
Raise aesthetic ambition beyond a competent generic AI frontend by establishing a project-specific visual world before implementation.

## 1. Read Product Meaning
Translate the product context into emotional and visual tensions. Identify what the service should feel like, not only what functions it contains.

## 2. Define Aesthetic Ambition
Before coding, establish internally:
- desired emotional impression,
- utility ↔ expression priority,
- what should feel familiar,
- what should feel unexpected,
- what should make this page recognizable as Jobiss rather than a generic career SaaS.

Do not use `clean, modern, professional` as the complete direction.

## 3. Reference Deconstruction
When references would materially help, inspect strong contemporary editorial, digital-product, identity, or portfolio work. Do not copy layouts. Extract reusable principles such as composition logic, typography behavior, image-to-type relationship, scale contrast, rhythm, framing, materiality, transitions, and one memorable visual device.

## 4. Commit to One Visual Thesis
Write one internal sentence connecting Jobiss's product meaning to visible design choices.

## 5. Design a World, Not a Component Collection
Avoid solving every section independently with another card, pill, icon row, glow, or rounded container. Treat typography, whitespace, image assets, composition, background, motion, and UI objects as one system.

## 6. Preserve Product Clarity
Art direction may use asymmetry, tension, unusual scale, overlap, controlled density, or deliberate friction, but the core value and primary action must remain understandable.

This protocol defines direction only. Post-render diagnosis and revision are handled separately by the experiment condition.
